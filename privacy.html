Privacy policy

The short version: Doodle Heroes has no accounts, no analytics, and no server of its own. Your drawings never leave your device. The written pages carry advertising, and that does involve Google — the detail is below.

Last updated 1 August 2026 · Applies to doodleheros.com

1. Who runs this site

Doodle Heroes is a free browser game published at doodleheros.com. Questions about this policy, or requests relating to your data, can be sent to hello@doodleheros.com.

2. What we collect ourselves

Nothing.

We want to be precise rather than merely reassuring, so concretely: the site has no backend server, no database, no user accounts, no login, no newsletter, no contact form, no analytics package, no tracking pixels, and no telemetry. We do not set cookies. We do not log visits. We do not know how many people play, what they draw, or what scores they get.

The site is a set of static files served from a content delivery network. Like any web host, that network processes the technical information your browser necessarily sends in order to deliver a page — such as your IP address and browser user agent — and may retain it briefly in operational logs for security and abuse prevention. We do not have access to those logs as an identifiable record of individual visitors, and we do not use them to build any profile.

3. Your drawings

When you draw a hero, the drawing is created and processed entirely within your browser, on your own device.

It is never uploaded, never transmitted, never stored on any server, and never seen by us or by any other person. There is no gallery, no sharing feature, no moderation queue, and no mechanism by which one player's drawing could reach another player.

Drawings are also not saved locally between sessions. When you close the tab, the hero is gone. If you want to keep one, take a screenshot — that is the only way.

4. Data stored on your device

The game does not use cookies, localStorage, or any other mechanism to store information about you on your device.

It does register a service worker, which saves a copy of the game's own files — the HTML, icons and stylesheet — in your browser's cache so the game can run offline. This cache contains only the game's public files. It holds no personal information, no drawings, and no identifiers. You can remove it at any time by clearing site data for doodleheros.com in your browser settings.

Third parties described below may set their own cookies or similar identifiers. Those are governed by their policies, not this one.

5. Advertising

The written pages on this site — the guide, the drawing tips, the parents' page and the about page — carry advertising supplied by Google AdSense. This is what pays for the domain and hosting.

The game itself carries no advertising. The title, drawing, play and results screens contain no ads of any kind.

Where ads are shown, Google as a third-party vendor may use cookies or similar technologies, and will receive information your browser sends as part of the ad request — including your IP address, user agent, and the page you are viewing. Google may use this to serve and measure ads and to detect fraud.

We have configured advertising on this site as follows:

  • Non-personalised ads are requested. Ads are not selected on the basis of a behavioural profile of the viewer. Non-personalised ads may still use contextual signals such as the content of the page and coarse location, and may still involve cookies or identifiers for frequency capping, aggregate reporting and fraud prevention.
  • The site is tagged for child-directed treatment. This instructs Google to restrict the categories of ads served and to disable interest-based targeting and remarketing for this site.
  • Ad slots are labelled so that advertising is visually distinguishable from our own content.

You can review and adjust how Google serves ads to you at myadcenter.google.com, and read how Google handles data from partner sites at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Using an ad blocker will not break the game.

6. Fonts

All pages, including the game, load two typefaces from Google Fonts. To deliver those files your browser makes a request to Google's servers, which necessarily reveals your IP address and user agent to Google. Google states that Google Fonts does not set cookies and does not use these requests for advertising or profiling.

We mention this because it is the one network request the game makes to a third party, and a privacy policy that said "the game contacts nobody" would be inaccurate. Once the game is cached for offline use, the fonts are cached too and no further requests are made.

7. Children's privacy

Doodle Heroes is intended to be suitable for children, and we treat the whole site as child-directed.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age. Because the site has no accounts, no contact form, no chat, no comments, no uploads, and no analytics, there is no route by which a child could submit personal information to us even if they tried. We therefore hold no children's personal information and have none to disclose, sell, or delete.

Consistent with the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the UK and EU rules on children's data, advertising on this site is tagged for child-directed treatment and is non-personalised, and no interest-based profiling or remarketing is permitted on it.

If you are a parent or guardian and you believe your child has somehow provided personal information to us, please contact hello@doodleheros.com and we will investigate and respond.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, data protection law may give you the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data, to object to processing, to data portability, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information.

We are able to honour these rights trivially, because we hold no personal data about you. There is no account to close, no profile to delete, and no record of your visit for us to produce. We do not sell or share personal information, and we never have.

Rights relating to data processed by Google in connection with advertising must be exercised with Google, using the links in section 5. If you want to raise something with us, write to hello@doodleheros.com and we will help where we can.

9. Links to other sites

Advertisements link to third-party websites that we do not operate and do not control. This policy does not apply to them, and we are not responsible for their content or privacy practices. We would encourage parents to be aware that any ad-supported site can, in principle, link a child outward to somewhere unfamiliar.

10. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Since we hold no user data, there is no store of personal information that could be breached — a design choice as much as a security measure.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with an updated date at the top. Material changes — for example, if we ever began collecting information or added a feature that transmitted drawings — would be described plainly here rather than buried.

12. Contact

Questions, corrections, or concerns about this policy: hello@doodleheros.com.
